Sponsor a Project

Partner with us to bring lasting change to communities in arid and semi-arid regions. By sponsoring a project, you directly help families access clean water, grow more food, restore their environment, and build sustainable livelihoods. Each project you support creates long-term impact and empowers communities to become more resilient and self-reliant.

Sand Dam

A reinforced concrete wall built across a seasonal riverbed to trap sand and store water within it. It provides a clean, reliable, year-round water source that resists evaporation and contamination.

Shallow Well

A man-made well accessing shallow groundwater (under 30m depth). Protected and well-maintained, these provide communities with a convenient and reliable water source for household use and farming.

School Water Tank

A 104,000-liter reinforced concrete storage structure within a school compound designed to harvest, store, and supply clean water for drinking, sanitation, and general use throughout the year.

Rock Catchment

A rainwater harvesting system using natural rock surfaces to channel runoff into large storage tanks. These maximize existing natural formations to harvest significant volumes of water.

Solar Pipeline Project

A sustainable solution using solar-powered pumps to distribute water from sand dams or boreholes through a pipe network. It reduces the physical burden of fetching water and supports irrigation.

Our Trainings

Deliver essential skills in sustainable agriculture, group savings, WASH, and climate-smart practices. This empowers communities to increase food production, grow incomes, and build long-term resilience.

Your contribution is an investment in sustainable development. Support a project and empower communities to thrive for generations.
