Environmental Conservation & NRM

Restoring ecosystems and building climate resilience through nature-based solutions

Overview

Deforestation and land degradation worsen droughts and reduce the effectiveness of water infrastructure. ASDF's environmental programme works to reverse this trend through large-scale tree planting and natural resource management (NRM).

What We Do

We plant indigenous trees on hillsides and riverbanks surrounding sand dam catchments. We also train communities on soil conservation, rotational grazing and watershed management.

  • Indigenous tree nurseries and mass planting
  • Hillside terracing and soil bunds
  • Riverbank stabilisation
  • Community-based NRM committees
  • Carbon sequestration monitoring

Our Focus Areas

Within our environmental and natural resource management work, communities take part in three core, practical programmes:

Agro-Forestry

We integrate indigenous and fruit-bearing trees into farmland so that crops, livestock and woodland thrive together. Agro-forestry restores soil fertility, provides shade and fodder, diversifies household income and locks carbon back into the landscape.

Energy Stoves

We train households to build fuel-efficient cooking stoves that use far less firewood than open fires. This cuts the time and distance women and children spend collecting fuel, reduces indoor smoke, and eases pressure on surrounding forests.

Farm Terracing

On the steep hillsides of Makueni, Machakos and Kitui we help communities construct terraces and soil bunds that slow run-off, trap moisture and stop fertile topsoil from washing away — turning fragile slopes into productive, climate-resilient farmland.

Our Impact

Over 1 million trees planted across Makueni, Machakos and Kitui counties. Rehabilitated landscapes show measurably reduced erosion, improved groundwater recharge and returning biodiversity.
