About Us

Home / About Us

Africa Sand Dam Foundation (ASDF) is a registered Kenyan NGO which was formed in 2010, working in Makueni, Machakos, and Kitui counties in the Republic of Kenya. The organisation's mission is to create food, water, and income-secure communities across the arid and semi-arid lands of Africa.
