Who We Are

Building food, water & income-secure communities

Africa Sand Dam Foundation (ASDF) is a registered Kenyan NGO working in Makueni, Machakos, and Kitui counties in the Republic of Kenya. Our mission is to create food, water, and income-secure communities across the arid and semi-arid lands of Africa.

We work hand in hand with community self-help groups to harvest and conserve soil and water — turning seasonal rivers and rainfall into a reliable, year-round resource that transforms lives for generations.

2010 Serving communities since

Our Vision

Soil and water conservation in drylands enables millions of rural communities to transform their own lives.

Our Mission

To support the resilience of community self-help groups (SHGs) through soil and water conservation to gain sustainable access to safe water, natural resources, food production and markets in the arid and semi-arid lands of Africa.

Our Reach

Where We Work

From our roots in Kenya, our work has grown across the drylands of Africa and beyond — implementing sand dams, rock catchments, school water tanks, solar-powered pipeline systems, soil and water conservation initiatives, and agroforestry activities.

Community-Led

A 50–50 partnership

Every project is built on a 50–50 partnership model. Community members contribute local materials and labour, while ASDF supplies the technical expertise and hardware materials.

This shared ownership ensures that the infrastructure communities help build is infrastructure they value, maintain, and sustain for generations to come.
