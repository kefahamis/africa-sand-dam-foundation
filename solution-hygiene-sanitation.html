Hygiene & Sanitation

Improving health outcomes through safe sanitation and hygiene behaviour change

Overview

Access to clean water alone is not enough. Without proper sanitation and hygiene practices, waterborne diseases remain common. ASDF's WASH programme addresses the full sanitation chain.

What We Do

We construct latrines, handwashing stations and school sanitation blocks, and run community hygiene education campaigns that change behaviour around water use, food handling and waste disposal.

  • School toilet and handwashing unit construction
  • Community latrine and sanitation block installation
  • Hygiene promotion and behaviour change training
  • Menstrual hygiene management in schools
  • Safe water storage and handling education

Our Impact

Communities report significant reductions in diarrhoeal disease following WASH interventions. School sanitation projects directly improve girls' attendance and retention rates.
