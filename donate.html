Why Give

Every shilling builds lasting water security

Africa Sand Dam Foundation works hand in hand with rural communities to harvest and conserve soil and water. A sand dam costs a fraction of conventional water infrastructure, lasts for generations, and is built and owned by the community itself. Your donation goes directly into projects on the ground in Makueni, Machakos and Kitui counties.
